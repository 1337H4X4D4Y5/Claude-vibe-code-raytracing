3D implementation of Li & Desbrun, SIGGRAPH 2023 (LD23). D3Q27 velocity LBM coupled to a D3Q7 Allen-Cahn phase-field, with moving-wall bounce-back, momentum-exchange forces and dead/fresh cell repopulation. Volume rendered with a WebGL2 raymarcher.
